FAQ

Do scans work on localhost?

If the QR encodes 127.0.0.1, only that device can reach it. For phones on your network, use your computer’s LAN IP or a tunnel; in production, use your public domain.

Can I delete QRs? What happens to files?

Deleting removes the record and invalidates the short link. The underlying file isn’t deleted from storage in the current version.

What if a QR points to an old domain?

Regenerate a new QR after changing the base URL/domain so scans reach the correct server.

How accurate are analytics?

We log per request to /s/<slug>. Some scanners prefetch; counts generally map 1:1 with real opens.

Can I customize frames and colors?

Frames are available in the editor; more customization options are on the roadmap.
