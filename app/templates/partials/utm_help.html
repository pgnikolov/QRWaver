What the UTM fields mean

utm_source is a required UTM parameter that identifies the origin of web traffic, such as the website, platform, or publication that a visitor came from. It is used to track and analyze marketing campaign performance, with examples including utm_source=google or utm_source=facebook. This parameter is crucial for understanding where your traffic originates from to inform marketing decisions.

utm_medium is a UTM parameter used to identify the marketing channel or type of link, such as email, cpc, social, or display, that brought traffic to a website. It is a required parameter, along with utm_source, and helps analytics tools categorize traffic to measure campaign effectiveness and optimize marketing efforts.

utm_campaign is a URL parameter used to identify the specific marketing campaign a link belongs to, helping marketers track performance in analytics tools like Google Analytics. It groups related links under a single campaign name, allowing for analysis of how different channels contribute to a campaign's goals, such as a "spring sale" or "product-demo".

utm_term is a specific UTM parameter used to identify the keyword or search term that a user clicked on in a paid search ad. It helps marketers track which specific keywords are driving traffic and conversions, providing more granular data for performance analysis in web analytics tools like Google Analytics. While most commonly used for paid search, it can also be used to identify other targeting details like a topic or audience.

utm_content is a UTM parameter used to identify what specific content or creative was used in a campaign to drive traffic to a website, such as a unique ad, email, or link. It helps differentiate between multiple similar links pointing to the same URL, making it useful for A/B testing and comparing the performance of different versions of content. For example, you might use utm_content=logolink for one version of an ad and utm_content=slogan1 for another, or utm_content=header_link and utm_content=footer_link for two different links in the same email.

Tip: UTMs are saved at creation and (for URL QRs) forwarded on redirect without overwriting any existing UTMs on the destination.
